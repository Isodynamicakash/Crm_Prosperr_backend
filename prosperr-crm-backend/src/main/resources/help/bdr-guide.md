# Prosperr Sales CRM: guide for BDRs

## What a BDR does in this CRM
Page: /
A BDR (business development rep) turns companies into qualified first meetings (L1) for an AE (account executive). The BDR owns everything up to and including the SQL (sales qualified lead). After a manager approves the SQL and assigns an AE, the AE runs the deal; the BDR can still see it and it counts on the BDR's scoreboard. Prosperr sells a compliant Flexible Benefits Plan that raises employees' in-hand salary at no extra payroll cost, so the buyers are CFOs, HR heads and payroll heads.

## The menu and keyboard shortcuts
Page: /
A BDR sees: Home, My Day, Accounts, My leads, SQLs, Sequences, Event QR and Tasks. Pipeline, Reports, Team and Settings are for managers. Keyboard shortcuts: press G, then a letter: G H Home, G D My Day, G A Accounts, G B My leads, G S SQLs, G E Sequences, G Q Event QR, G T Tasks. Hovering a menu item shows its shortcut. Press Cmd K (Ctrl K on Windows) or use the search box to find any company or person. The language picker at the bottom of the menu switches the app to 13 Indian languages. On a phone the menu is the bar at the bottom.

## Signing in and Google connection
Page: /
Sign in with your work email or Google. If your account is switched off by an admin, you are signed out within about 15 seconds. Use "Connect Google (Calendar + Gmail)" at the bottom of the menu once: it lets L1 meetings create a Google Calendar event with a Meet link and lets email sequences send from your own Gmail. Without it, meetings are still booked in the CRM and sequences cannot send.

## Home
Page: /
Home shows your numbers for the period you pick at the top: 7 days, 30 days, this month, quarter, any month (Month), or Custom dates. Tiles: calls, connect rate (share of calls where you reached the person), SQLs submitted and L1 booked. "My numbers vs target" shows L1 booked, L1 done, L2 done and L3 done against your targets, week by week (month by month for long periods). Targets are shared out by day, so a part of a month shows the part of the target; numbers are whole numbers. Below: activity over time, call outcomes, conversion, targets, "Up next" from your calling list, and your calling streak.

## Your coach (voice) on Home
Page: /
The "Your coach" card on Home plays a spoken review of the period you picked, in the language you choose: your L1s against target and pace (how many you need a day), your rank among BDRs, what is going well, up to three things to improve with an action each, and one focus for the week. It compares you with the team only when enough calls are logged. You can also type or speak a question to it, like "how many L1s do I need a day?". It only reads your data and never changes anything. Press Refresh to rebuild the review.

## My Day: the calling list
Page: /my-day
My Day is your list of whom to call, in order: Overdue (follow-ups past their time, call these first), Due today, New leads (your contacts never contacted, with no open task, most senior first), and Revival (your inactive companies whose revisit date has come). The voice card at the top, "Your day, spoken", plays a short brief of your day and answers "whom should I call first?". Press "Start session" to call one person at a time.

## Calling session
Page: /my-day
Start session opens one person at a time: their past calls and the latest notes on the company, a Call button and WhatsApp, then "Log what happened", which saves and moves to the next person on its own. Skip and Back move through the list; live tallies show calls, people reached and L1s booked, and you get a summary at the end. Keys: C call, L log, S skip, left arrow back, Esc end.

## Logging a call and what each outcome does
Page: /accounts
Log a call from the calling session or with "Log" next to a contact (or "Log activity" on the company). Pick the outcome; the form previews what will happen before you save.
- Did not pick, Switched off, Incoming barred: contact becomes Contacted; call again in 2 working days. After 6 tries the contact becomes Unresponsive and is revisited in 30 days; the CRM offers an email sequence.
- Call later, Call after a month: Follow-up pending; a follow-up on the date you give (a date is required).
- Asked details: Connected; "send template" task in 2 working days.
- Email sent: Connected; call again in 3 working days.
- Reminded over the phone: no change.
- Demo / L1 booked: Meeting scheduled; opens the SQL form; stops every email sequence at that company.
- Referred, Not a right stakeholder: Referred; call the new person in 1 working day; asks for the referred person's details.
- Not interested, Already using something: Not relevant; a reason is required; revisited in 90 days.
- Not possible, Not associated anymore: Not relevant.
- Wrong ICP: Not relevant and the company is marked ICP fit: No.
- Wrong number/invalid: that number is marked invalid and you get a "find a number" task.
AI help: speak, upload a voice note or type your notes in any Indian language and the AI fills the outcome, remark and follow-up date. Check it, then save.

## Contact status
Page: /accounts
A contact moves on its own: Not contacted, Contacted, Connected, Follow-up pending or Referred, Meeting scheduled, Meeting completed, or Not relevant / Unresponsive. Contacts marked Do not contact drop off calling lists and their sequences stop.

## Accounts page: Mine, Worked by me, Everyone
Page: /accounts
Mine = companies you are assigned to. Worked by me = companies you have called, emailed, noted, booked or submitted, newest first. Everyone = your prospects plus the team pipeline. Rows show "You · 3 calls · 2d ago" and "Also worked by …". Filter by status, zone, stage, size, temperature, city; save a view; Export to download. A company page shows "Already worked by": who touched it, how often and when, so two BDRs don't chase the same people. A contact owned by another BDR shows as "Another BDR's contact" with no phone or actions.

## Adding a company (New account)
Page: /accounts
Accounts, New account (or New account on Home). Enter name, website, city, industry, employees, licence potential and lead source. The website domain is checked first: if the company is already in the CRM you join it instead of creating a copy, and you are told which people other BDRs already work there.

## Adding contacts (stakeholders)
Page: /accounts
On a company, press Stakeholder (or Add in the Stakeholders card). Enter full name, designation, function (Finance, HR, Payroll, Other), emails, phones, LinkedIn, referred by, main contact (primary SPOC) and decision maker. AI can fill the form from speech or pasted text. The designation sets the level: L1 = CXO, VP and heads (CFO, CHRO, VP Finance), L2 = directors and controllers, down to L5. A title not on the list has no level until a manager maps it. If the same email, LinkedIn or phone already exists anywhere, you are told who owns that person instead of creating a duplicate. Adding several people at once: "Adding several people? Upload a CSV" (up to 200) with columns Name, Designation, Email, Phone, LinkedIn; "Sample CSV" downloads a ready file.

## Importing leads from Excel, CSV or Apollo
Page: /accounts
Accounts, Import. Choose "Excel or CSV" or "Apollo export". Steps: choose the file (and the sheet in a workbook), match columns, check, done. Up to 5,000 rows and 20 MB per file. "Download sample CSV" gives a file with the right headings: Company, Website, City, Industry, Employees, Licences, Lead source, First name, Last name, Designation, Department, Email, Phone, LinkedIn, BDR email, Remarks; paste your leads under them and delete the example rows. Required: Company and a name. Other headings like Mobile, Job title, Organisation are matched automatically and you can change any match. Small differences are understood: Bangalore or Gurgaon, "Fintech" or "Information Technology", "Human Resources", "1.2k" or "501-1,000" employees. The check step shows each row's result before anything is saved: New company, Joins existing company, Check first (phone matches someone), Already in CRM (skipped), Has a problem (skipped). Then Import, and download the report. In Apollo: select people, Export, CSV; lead source is set to Apollo.

## Scanning a visiting card
Page: /accounts
Accounts, Scan card (or "Scan visiting card" inside Add stakeholder). Take a photo or choose one (JPG or PNG). In about 5 seconds the name, title, company, emails, phones, website and city are filled in. A QR code on the card is read first (exact). Unsure fields are highlighted; "checks on what was read" lists any repairs; it may suggest a matching company or title already in the CRM ("Use it"). If the person is already in the CRM you are told and can open their record. Nothing is saved until you press Save contact; new companies get lead source Event.

## Event QR (lead capture at events)
Page: /event-qr
Event QR, New event QR, give the event's name (people see it). Optionally switch on the welcome email, sent from your Gmail to new contacts only. Show full screen turns the screen into a big QR with the event and your name; the screen stays awake and shows new leads as they arrive. Poster to print downloads an A5 poster; QR only downloads the code; Copy link copies the form link. People scan with their phone camera and fill name, company, title, work email, phone and a consent tick. Each lead becomes your contact with lead source Event, a follow-up task for the next working morning at 11, a notification and a note. Someone already in the CRM is matched, never duplicated, and the follow-up goes to whoever owns them. See leads shows each one as New contact, Already in CRM or Check this (it could not be added automatically, for example the company is in an AE's pipeline; add it from Accounts). Switch "QR is live" off after the event.

## My leads page
Page: /leads
My leads shows only your own companies and contacts. Pick a period (7 days, 30 days, this month, quarter, any month, custom). "What you did" shows L1 booked and L1 done against target, calls and connect rate, SQLs, an activity chart (daily, weekly or monthly) and call outcomes. "Where your leads stand right now": contacts step by step (all, reached out, had a conversation, meeting booked, meeting held), companies by stage, lead source, ICP city fit, company size, seniority and functions. "What kind of company converts" groups companies by industry, city, size or lead source, split by stage, for you or the whole team, and names the best and worst converters. "Needs your attention" lists quiet companies that matter most. "Your leads" is the full list: search, filter by status and level, sort, and "Only worked in this period". Calls from the team's sheets that had no date are not counted in any period.

## The SQL form: the four checks
Page: /sql
Submit an SQL with "Submit SQL" on a company or when you log Demo / L1 booked. The form runs four checks live; all four must pass, or a manager must approve the exception.
- Licences (taxable bracket): 251 or more passes. 250 or fewer needs a reason and a manager's approval, and at most 20% of your approved SQLs in a month can be small.
- Location: P0 cities pass (Bengaluru, Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Faridabad, Ghaziabad). P1 cities (Chennai, Mumbai) need High intent, a reason and manager confirmation. Other cities fail.
- Right stakeholder: an L1 or L2 reached directly. L3 to L5 only if referred by an L1 or L2.
- BANT: all four filled.
If the company has no city or licence count, the form says so and offers "Fill in now", which opens Edit account.

## BANT and the L1 booking in the SQL
Page: /sql
Budget: cost-neutral fit or budget available. Authority: sign-off owner identified or not yet known. Need: flexible benefits pain or payroll pain. Timeline: this quarter, next quarter, later. Each has a note. The SQL also records business relevance (FBP, Payroll or both), intent (Low, Medium, High with a reason) and the L1 booking: date, time, length, meeting link and attendees. AI can fill BANT from your notes or a voice note.

## After you submit an SQL
Page: /sql
The SQLs page shows where every SQL stands. Status starts as Meeting booked and the company moves to the pipeline. A manager reviews it: Approved makes it a Qualified SQL, creates the deal and assigns an AE (the least-loaded AE is recommended). Rejected (with a reason such as below licence threshold, outside ICP cities, wrong stakeholder, BANT not convincing, duplicate) cancels its open meetings, takes the company out of the pipeline and gives you a task saying why. You get a notification either way.

## Meetings, Google Calendar, reschedules and no-shows
Page: /accounts
An L1 booked with the SQL creates a Google Calendar event with a Meet link for the booker (or the AE if you are not connected); you see your busy times while booking. You can reschedule a meeting you booked from the company page: the calendar event moves and keeps its link. Only the AE records the outcome, and only after the meeting time. A no-show gives you, the booker, a reschedule task.

## After the SQL: the deal is view only for BDRs
Page: /accounts
Once the SQL is approved the deal belongs to the AE. On the company page the Opportunity card says "View only": the AE ticks the stage requirements and records meeting outcomes; you can follow every step and it still counts on your scoreboard. Stages: SQL, L1 done, L2, L3, Negotiation, Won or Lost. Each stage has a checklist; the step marked "Needed to move on" needs a file or link. Ticks marked "From the sheets" were filled from the team's sheets for deals that had already passed that stage.

## Email sequences
Page: /sequences
Start one with "Sequence" (Start sequence) on any contact with an email. Three emails from your own Gmail, then a reminder to call: day 0 intro (pitch by the contact's function), day 2 follow-up and day 5 final follow-up in the same thread, day 7 a "call them" task. You see a preview of every email with its send date first. Sending rules: weekdays 9:30 to 6:30 IST, at most 80 a day, at least 90 seconds apart; you are warned if two people at one company would get an email the same day. A sequence stops on its own on a reply (contact becomes Connected and you get a call task), a bounce (address marked invalid), an unsubscribe (do not contact) or an L1 booked at that company. After 3 failed sends it pauses and shows an alert (usually Google needs reconnecting). The Sequences page has Running, Replied and Ended tabs with Pause, Resume and Stop. It needs Google connected.

## Tasks
Page: /tasks
Tasks lists your follow-ups and next steps: from call outcomes, sequences, meetings, rejections, Event QR leads and handovers. Mark done, move to tomorrow 10 AM, change the date, or add a New task for any company.

## Notifications
Page: /
The bell at the top shows notifications: SQL approved or rejected, accounts assigned to you, new Event QR leads, unresponsive main contacts and more. Click one to open the record.

## Inactive companies and revival
Page: /my-day
A company that is not moving can be made Inactive with a reason and a revisit date. When the date comes it appears under Revival in My Day; calling or adding a new contact brings it back. Inactive is a status: nothing in the pipeline is deleted.

## When a BDR leaves or goes on leave
Page: /accounts
A manager hands their companies over from the Team page: balanced by load (least-loaded BDR first), round-robin, or all to one person, with a review step. You then get each company in Mine with its contacts, open tasks, upcoming meetings and paused sequences (resume them from your own Gmail), plus a notification. Their past calls and notes stay on the timeline.

## Targets and how they are counted
Page: /
Targets per BDR are monthly and weekly (from the team's sheets): L1 booked, L1 done, L3 done. A target is shared out by day: a whole month shows the monthly figure; 30 days across two months adds each month's share. L1 booked counts by the date it was booked; L1 done by the meeting date. Shown as whole numbers.

## Data that came from the team's sheets
Page: /leads
Companies, contacts, calls, notes, meetings, deals and targets were loaded from the team's Excel sheets. Rows with no date in the sheet show "No date in sheet" and are not counted in any period. Meeting times in the sheets were not recorded, so imported meetings show 11:00.

## Common problems
Page: /
- "I can't see someone's phone": they are another BDR's contact. Talk to that BDR or your manager.
- "I can't tick the deal checklist": after the SQL is approved the deal is the AE's; it is view only for BDRs.
- "Submit SQL is blocked": one of the four checks fails; the form says which. Use "Fill in now" for a missing city or licence count.
- "My sequence is paused": Google needs reconnecting, 3 sends failed, or the sequence was handed over to you; reconnect Google and press Resume.
- "It says the person already exists": the email, LinkedIn or phone is already in the CRM; open their record instead.
- "The voice or AI buttons are missing": AI is switched off on this server, or you reached the limit of 60 AI requests an hour.
- "The import skipped rows": open the report; rows already in the CRM or with a problem (like an invalid email) are skipped.
- "A city or industry was not found in import": use the CRM's names or the sample CSV; most common spellings are understood.
